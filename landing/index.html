How it works

  1. 1

    Connect your model

    Bring your own API key or a local model. Rakazo talks to it over a standard interface — swap models whenever you like.

  2. 2

    Pick a sandbox

    Give the bot a real computer: a Docker sandbox it can browse from, run commands on, and work with files in.

  3. 3

    Give it a job

    Start from a template or describe the job in plain Markdown. The bot runs it and pings you when it needs a decision.

Run it on your own machine.

No account required, no data leaving your network unless you say so. Rakazo is a normal app you deploy: Docker and Postgres, and you're up.

Your own keys

Bring any model — hosted APIs or local weights. Your keys live in your environment, never on someone else's server.

Your own data

Threads, memory, and history live in Postgres on your box. Export or wipe it any time; nothing is phoned home.

Routines in plain Markdown

A bot's job is just a Markdown file you can read, edit, and version-control. No black-box prompts.

Approvals + audit log

Risky actions pause for your approval. Every tool call is written to an audit log you can inspect later.
